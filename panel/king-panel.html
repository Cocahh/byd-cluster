Blue is electric, amber is fuelThe mode, power bar and stores turn amber when the 1.5 engine joins in, so you can see the hybrid working at a glance.
Power, not revsThe bar runs from charging on the left to full power on the right. The mark is zero. Braking and lifting off fill it to the left.
Safety lights stay BYD'sSeat belt, airbag, brake and engine warnings stay on the King's own display. This design keeps a red row free for anything the car reports.
